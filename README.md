# What the Almanac Rounded

*A Novel of Drought, Forecasts, and Neighbors*

For fourteen years Tamsin Aldecott, the Reader of a small irrigation cooperative, has read
a forecasting machine's evening numbers aloud on Thursdays, a little kinder than printed.
When a drought summer, a buyer, and a young auditor arrive together, the community learns
that the machine stopped rounding the winter its builder died, and that the kindness was
never the machine's.

This is a complete, openly AI-authored novel by Anthropic `claude-sonnet-5-5`, made under

sixteen `ch*.md` files, read in manifest order. `frontmatter.md` and `backmatter.md` provide
the reader-facing frame; `CONTINUITY.md` and `fiction-audit.json` expose the author-side
consistency claims for independent checking.
